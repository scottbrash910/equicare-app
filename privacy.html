Privacy at Equicare Gezira

Equicare Gezira Riding Center, Gezira Sporting Club, Zamalek, Cairo · Updated 18 November 2026

What we collect

When you use the Equicare app or message us on Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp, we keep what you give us: the rider's name, Gezira membership number, date of birth, phone number, riding level, bookings and payments, and the messages you send us.

Why

To book and manage lessons, answer your questions, send lesson reminders and keep the riding center safe and organised. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising outside Equicare.

Messages on Instagram and Facebook

Messages you send to our Instagram or Facebook accounts are answered by the Equicare team with help from an automated assistant. To write a reply, the text of the conversation is processed by our AI provider (Anthropic), which does not use it to train its models. A team member can take over any chat; just ask.

Who can see it

Equicare staff, and the services that run our app and messages for us (Supabase for the database, Vercel for hosting, Meta for Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp, Anthropic for the assistant). Each of them only handles the information needed for their part.

How long we keep it

Rider records stay while the rider rides with us and are kept as the club's records afterwards. Chat messages are kept for up to 12 months.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or ask us to delete your chat history, at any time: message us, or email equicare.geziraclub@gmail.com.
